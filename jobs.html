Working at Cedar Grove

Cedar Grove is a small, not-for-profit school set on an organic farm in Prince Edward County. Our teachers and staff work closely together, with small classes, a strong sense of community, and daily life woven into the land — the forest, the garden, and the animals we care for.

We look for people who bring warmth, curiosity, and a genuine respect for childhood to their work — whether that’s in the classroom, in the garden, or supporting our families and community from behind the scenes. Experience with Waldorf or nature-based education is welcome but not required; what matters most is a willingness to learn, collaborate, and grow alongside our students.

How to Apply

We accept resumes and expressions of interest on an ongoing basis, even when a specific position isn’t posted. Send us your resume and a short note about what draws you to Cedar Grove, and we’ll be in touch if there’s a fit.

Looking to share a specialty skill — French, music, visual arts, carpentry, knitting and handwork, outdoor and wilderness skills, and more — rather than a full-time role? Visit our Faculty & Staff page to learn more about joining as a specialty teacher.
