A 5% sibling discount

Families with more than one child enrolled at Cedar Grove receive 5% off tuition. It is applied automatically, so there is nothing to ask for.

A not-for-profit school

Cedar Grove is a registered not-for-profit. Tuition covers the real cost of running a small school with an 8:1 ratio, and nothing beyond it.

Paying for the year

Tuition can be paid as a single lump sum, or spread evenly across ten monthly payments, whichever suits your household better.

Places are limited

Our ratio is the reason we keep classes small, and the reason places fill early. We enrol JK through Grade 5.

Questions about cost

If tuition is the thing standing between your family and Cedar Grove, please write to us at Admin@cedargroveschool.ca. We would rather have the conversation.
